Research

My research in oncology and immuno-oncology has produced peer-reviewed publications, manuscripts under review, and presentations at the field's leading conferences — ASH, ASCO, ASHG, and AACR. That same rigor for interrogating primary data and biological mechanism is what I bring to biotech and life sciences equity research: ready to dig into the science behind the next investment thesis.
