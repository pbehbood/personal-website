01 — About

I'm a PhD-trained bioengineer with a focus on translational science, advanced in vitro models, and data-driven biological evaluation. My work spans organ-on-chip platforms, functional genomics, and multi-omic data integration — from engineering 3D microfluidic disease models at the bench to mining large patient datasets for prognostic biomarkers and therapeutic targets.

Across academic, industry, and postdoctoral settings, I've learned to move fluidly between generating experimental data and communicating what it means — to clinicians, statisticians, and non-technical stakeholders alike. I'm currently expanding that translational lens into biotech and medtech investment through scientific due diligence work.

02 — Experience

  1. Mar 2026 — Present

    Business Development Fellow

    H7 BioCapital · Remote

    • Conduct scientific due diligence on healthcare opportunities, evaluating intellectual property, competitive landscape, and market potential to support biotech and medtech investment decisions.
  2. May 2024 — Present

    Postdoctoral Fellow, Genomics

    Center for Discovery and Innovation (CDI), Hackensack Meridian Health

    • Direct large-scale epigenetic analysis of ~150 multiple myeloma patient samples, integrating methylation, copy-number, and gene-expression data to identify prognostic biomarkers and therapeutic liabilities.
    • Use allele-specific methylation mapping and multiplexed CRISPR mutagenesis to identify functional SNPs at post-GWAS cancer and autoimmune risk loci.
    • Established a framework linking inherited genetic predisposition to clinical progression in myeloma, showing acquired epigenetic changes at risk loci drive tumor evolution.
    • Elucidated epigenetic pathways underlying PFOS-induced multiple myeloma susceptibility in exposed plasma cells.
    • Synthesize findings into manuscripts, grant proposals, and conference presentations; collaborate closely with clinicians, statisticians, and external academic partners.
  3. Jan 2023 — Aug 2023

    PhD R&D Co-Op, Human Systems

    Regeneron Pharmaceuticals

    • Developed a 3D microfluidic Liver-Chip for disease modeling and drug screening in MAFLD and MASH, measuring liver enzymes and disease-relevant biomarkers.
    • Validated physiological relevance of the chip via LPS-mediated inflammatory response.
    • Screened a lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-encapsulated siRNA therapeutic candidate, assessing target gene knockdown, pathway modulation, and beneficial effects on the disease model.
    • Generated and analyzed experimental datasets that informed go/no-go decisions, communicating results to stakeholders through concise slide decks and data summaries.
  4. Sep 2019 — May 2025

    PhD Researcher, Organ-on-Chip Lab

    Stevens Institute of Technology

    • Discovered osteoblast-driven signaling mechanisms in the bone marrow microenvironment that promote multiple myeloma proliferation and therapy resistance, using integrated transcriptomic and proteomic profiling.
    • Developed a 3D microfluidic model simulating ascites-induced pressure in ovarian cancer, linked to elevated metastatic risk.
    • Enhanced NK cell cytotoxicity for adoptive cell therapy in multiple myeloma by modulating interactions between myeloma cells and non-hematopoietic stromal cells.
    • Regularly presented findings to technical and non-technical audiences, from lab meetings to national conferences.
